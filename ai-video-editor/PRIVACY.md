# Privacy Policy: Sagar Uploader

Sagar Uploader is a small personal tool used by one person, the owner of the YouTube channel
[@Sagarkekrishna](https://www.youtube.com/@Sagarkekrishna), to upload the owner's own videos to that channel.

**What it accesses.** Through the YouTube Data API it can upload videos, set thumbnails and read public trending
video information, only for the owner's own channel and only after the owner approved access with Google.

**What it collects.** It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person. It has no
users other than its owner, no website sign-up, no analytics and no advertising.

**What it stores.** A login token that lets the tool act on the owner's own channel is kept in a private place
that only the owner controls.

**Your control.** The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
The tool follows the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Contact.** Use the contact details on the channel page https://www.youtube.com/@Sagarkekrishna.

Last updated: 21 November 2026.
