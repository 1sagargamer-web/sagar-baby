# Sound effects library (CC0)

From the Hugging Face dataset `flreey/sfxmint-cc0-sound-effects` (sample folder), made by SFXMint and released
under **CC0 1.0** (free for any use, commercial included, no attribution needed). The sounds are AI-generated
(Stable Audio). sfxmint.com itself is not reachable from the cloud container; the 294 samples on Hugging Face are.
Trimmed/levelled copies used in projects are named `r-*.wav` (see `projects/demo-intro/assets/sfx/`).
